About ModuDesk

ModuDesk started in a small apartment where one desk had to be a workspace, a drawing table, and a dinner table. We built a desk from parts that could be rearranged, and friends kept asking for one.

Today we design modular desks that adapt to how you work. Every part uses the same clip system, so you can buy what you need now and add more later.

What we believe

Fewer parts, more uses

Every module works with every base.

Repair over replace

Spare parts stay available for years.

Honest materials

Birch plywood and steel. No hidden fillers.
